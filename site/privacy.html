This policy explains what personal information [LEGAL ENTITY] ("Byte Me Studios", "we", "us") collects when you interact with our services, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can do about it.

We have written this to be readable. Where we had a choice between the vague phrasing that is conventional in privacy policies and a specific statement of what actually happens, we chose the specific one.

Who this covers

  • Callers — people who phone a business using our AI receptionist.
  • Message recipients — people who receive or send SMS through our platform.
  • Customers — businesses that purchase our products.
  • Readers — people who buy our publications.

If you buy something

Payment is processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number. We retain the transaction amount, currency, date, a Stripe reference identifier, and — if you provide it — your email address.

What we do not collect

We do not track you across other websites, buy personal data from brokers, build advertising profiles, or use cookies for advertising.

How we use it

  • To do the thing you asked for: book your appointment, answer your question, fulfil your purchase.
  • To honor opt-outs, which requires keeping a record that you opted out.
  • To improve the service — reviewing what worked and what didn't.
  • To meet legal obligations, including proving consent for messaging.

We do not sell your personal information.

About the AI providers, specifically

The content of your messages and calls is sent to a third-party AI model provider to generate a response. This is how the service works, and we would rather say it plainly than bury it.

We do not send them your phone number, name, or payment information alongside that content. Providers may retain content briefly for abuse monitoring under their own policies. We do not permit our providers to train models on this content, and we will change providers rather than accept terms that allow it.

We may also disclose information where legally required, or to protect someone's safety.

If you are in California

The CCPA/CPRA gives you the right to know what we collect, delete it, correct it, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. Use the contact address above.

If you are in the EU or UK

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our legal bases are consent (for marketing messages), contract performance (for bookings and purchases), and legitimate interests (for operating and securing the service). You may complain to your supervisory authority.

Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Access to personal data is restricted to server-side systems using service-credentials; our database denies client access by default. We use encrypted connections throughout. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise — if a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

International transfers

Our processors operate in the United States. If you contact us from elsewhere, your information is transferred there and handled under this policy.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be announced before taking effect.
