IR Camera Detector

Most phone camera sensors can pick up a bit of near-infrared light that your eyes can't see, even though the screen shows normal colors. This tool boosts that signal so you can spot IR sources in the dark — a remote control's IR LED, or the infrared illuminators used by many night-vision security cameras.

Works best in a dark room, with a phone that doesn't have a strong IR-cut filter. Point it at your TV/AC remote and press a button as a quick test.
