Our products use AI. **AI systems make mistakes.** Ours may misunderstand a
caller, mis-transcribe a name, book a wrong time, or generate a reply that is
imperfect for your business.

We build guards against the worst of these — appointments cannot double-book,
the system cannot offer a time it wasn't given, and certain topics are escalated
to a human automatically. Guards reduce failures; they do not eliminate them.

**Review the output.** Do not deploy these systems into situations where an
error would cause serious harm.
