# Privacy Policy

**Byte Me Studios**
Last updated: [DATE] · Effective: [DATE]

This policy explains what personal information Arthur Trey Maloy, doing business as Byte Me Studios ("Byte Me
Studios", "we", "us") collects when you interact with our services, why we
collect it, who we share it with, and what you can do about it.

We have written this to be readable. Where we had a choice between the vague
phrasing that is conventional in privacy policies and a specific statement of
what actually happens, we chose the specific one.

## Who this covers

- **Callers** — people who phone a business using our AI receptionist.
- **Message recipients** — people who receive or send SMS through our platform.
- **Customers** — businesses that purchase our products.
- **Readers** — people who buy our publications.

## What we collect

### If you call a business using our receptionist

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your phone number | To identify your call and return it if needed |
| A text transcript of the call | To book correctly, and so the business can review what was said |
| Your name and reason for calling, if you give them | To book the appointment |
| Appointment date and time | To hold your booking |
| Call time, duration, and outcome | To operate and audit the service |

**We do not record or store call audio.** We store a text transcript produced by
our telephony provider.

### If you receive SMS from us

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your phone number | To send the messages you asked for |
| Your consent status, and when and how you gave it | To prove we had permission, and to honor opt-outs |
| Message content, both directions | To hold a conversation and keep a record |
| Delivery status | To know whether messages arrived |
| Any segment or interest information you provide | To send relevant messages rather than irrelevant ones |

### If you buy something

Payment is processed by Stripe. **We never receive or store your full card
number.** We retain the transaction amount, currency, date, a Stripe reference
identifier, and — if you provide it — your email address.

### What we do not collect

We do not track you across other websites, buy personal data from brokers,
build advertising profiles, or use cookies for advertising.

## How we use it

- To do the thing you asked for: book your appointment, answer your question,
  fulfil your purchase.
- To honor opt-outs, which requires keeping a record that you opted out.
- To improve the service — reviewing what worked and what didn't.
- To meet legal obligations, including proving consent for messaging.

**We do not sell your personal information.**

## Who we share it with

We use third-party processors to run the service. Each receives only what it
needs.

| Processor | What it receives | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Twilio** | Phone numbers, message content, call audio and transcripts | Sending and receiving SMS; connecting and transcribing calls |
| **Stripe** | Payment details you enter, transaction metadata | Processing payments |
| **Supabase** | All service data listed above | Database hosting |
| **AI model providers** (currently OpenRouter and Groq) | Message and call transcript content | Generating replies |

### About the AI providers, specifically

**The content of your messages and calls is sent to a third-party AI model
provider to generate a response.** This is how the service works, and we would
rather say it plainly than bury it.

We do not send them your phone number, name, or payment information alongside
that content. Providers may retain content briefly for abuse monitoring under
their own policies. We do not permit our providers to train models on this
content, and we will change providers rather than accept terms that allow it.

We may also disclose information where legally required, or to protect someone's
safety.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Retained |
|---|---|
| Short-term interaction logs | 24 hours, then deleted automatically |
| Summarized long-term records | Until you ask us to delete them |
| Consent and opt-out records | Indefinitely — we are required to prove we honored an opt-out |
| Call transcripts | [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 12 months] |
| Appointment records | [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24 months] |
| Payment records | 7 years, as tax law requires |

**Opt-out records are the deliberate exception.** If you tell us to stop
messaging you, we keep the record of that instruction permanently. Deleting it
would mean we could message you again by accident, which is the opposite of
what you asked for.

## Your choices

**Stop SMS at any time.** Reply **STOP** to any message. This takes effect
immediately and is permanent — our system will not re-subscribe you
automatically, even if you later text START. To genuinely resume, contact us.

**Get help.** Reply **HELP** to any message.

**Ask what we hold, or ask us to delete it.** Email bytemedevstudio@gmail.com. We will
respond within 30 days. We may need to verify you control the phone number or
email in question. Note that we cannot delete opt-out records or records we are
required to keep for tax or legal reasons.

**Correct something wrong.** Same address.

### If you are in California

The CCPA/CPRA gives you the right to know what we collect, delete it, correct
it, and not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell
or share personal information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to
opt out of on that front. Use the contact address above.

### If you are in the EU or UK

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction,
portability, and objection. Our legal bases are consent (for marketing
messages), contract performance (for bookings and purchases), and legitimate
interests (for operating and securing the service). You may complain to your
supervisory authority.

## Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly
collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information,
contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Access to personal data is restricted to server-side systems using
service-credentials; our database denies client access by default. We use
encrypted connections throughout. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not
claim otherwise — if a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law
requires.

## International transfers

Our processors operate in the United States. If you contact us from elsewhere,
your information is transferred there and handled under this policy.

## Changes

We will post changes here with a new "last updated" date. Material changes
affecting how we use your information will be announced before taking effect.
